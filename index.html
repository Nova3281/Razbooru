Third-party site disclaimer

The default image board in Razbooru is curated to only contain images that follow Google Play's Developer Content Policy. Razbooru (like most web browsing applications) allows the user to enter the addresses of third-party websites outside of the application (or its developers) control. Razbooru contains a rating filter, but this relies upon how the websites themselves rate their content. Due to this the developers of Razbooru can not be held liable if content retrieved from third-party websites is offensive or illegal in your territory.
